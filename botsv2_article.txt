35 What is the average Shannon entropy score of the subdomain containing UDP-exfiltrated data? Answer guidance: Cut off, not rounded, to the first decimal place. Answer examples: 3.2 or 223234.9
Let’s filter DNS.
Query:
index=”botsv2″ sourcetype=stream:dns dest_port=53
| stats count by dest_ip
| sort –count
Based from the result we got 2 possible external IP candidates for exfil. The rest of top IP are internal IPs.
Query:
index=botsv2 sourcetype=stream:dns (dest_ip=216.69.185.42 OR dest_ip=208.109.255.42) query=*
| table dest_ip query
We seem to be on the right track. Next let us parse the subdomain in order to calculate to entropy (degree of randomness) later.
Query:
index=botsv2 sourcetype=stream:dns (dest_ip=216.69.185.42 OR dest_ip=208.109.255.42) query=*
| rex field=query “(?<subdom>\w+).hildegardsfarm.com”
| table dest_ip subdom
Then using this guide
Query:
index=botsv2 sourcetype=stream:dns (dest_ip=216.69.185.42 OR dest_ip=208.109.255.42) query=*
| rex field=query “(?<subdom>\w+).hildegardsfarm.com”
| `ut_shannon(subdom)`
| stats avg(ut_shannon) by dest_ip

Answer:  3.6
